Make a Biotron clip

Biotron turns changes at the plant contacts into notes. This page makes the sound.

Try a repeatable example without a device. With Biotron, record your own performance.

1. Get your sound

The example uses fixed notes. Plant notes vary with the input.

After playing the example, you can play your computer keyboard: A S D F G H J K.

2. Record a take

Record sound for your editor, or try camera and sound in one file.

Use headphones if you include speech. Sound-only takes contain this synth.

Ready. Each take lasts up to 30 seconds.

Listen before saving. Wait for the sound to fade before stopping. Files stay here until reload; save useful takes.

3. Finish your video

A short shoot you can adapt
  1. Show the plant, contacts and Biotron in the same shot.
  2. Show a touch and let the music be heard.
  3. Say what creates the notes and where the sound comes from.
  4. Show the setup someone needs to try it.

Choose your own story. A separate soundtrack does not prove the visible touch caused each note.

Use sound in your usual editor
  1. Save a useful take.
  2. Open it in your editor alongside your camera video.
  3. Keep speech audible. Align the take if you want matching gestures.
  4. Export and open the final video outside this page.
